Analytical Banking Platform

Banking intelligence prototype for behavioral profiling, anomaly detection, and suspicious activity monitoring.

GitHub Demo Notes

Problem Statement

Financial institutions need early visibility into unusual behavior, suspicious activity, and risk patterns hidden in transaction data. This platform demonstrates an analytics workflow for detecting anomalies and summarizing risk signals for operational teams.

Dataset

The platform is designed for transaction-level banking data including customer IDs, transaction amounts, timestamps, merchant or channel categories, location signals, balance movement, and historical behavior windows.

Methodology

The workflow combines sequence modeling with LSTM for temporal behavior, Isolation Forest for unsupervised anomaly detection, and Random Forest for supervised risk classification. Dashboard views translate model outputs into alerts, customer behavior summaries, and trend reports.

Results

Detection output: anomaly scores, flagged transactions, and suspicious behavior categories.
Analytics output: customer behavior profiles, risk summaries, and monitoring dashboards.
Business value: earlier risk visibility and stronger evidence for investigation workflows.
